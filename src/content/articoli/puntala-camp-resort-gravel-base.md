Riffing on the famous slogan "Would you buy a used car from this man?", one might ask: "Would you ever sleep in a campsite that calls itself a resort?" PuntAla Camp & Resort is precisely that paradox—**a refuge in an oasis of beauty.** While "campsite" would be reductive and "resort" misleading, it transcends both labels through its dedication to outdoor culture, landscape stewardship, tangible sustainability, and love of adventure.

## Where it is

Located **between Punta Ala and Cala Civette, near the renowned Cala Violina**—one of Italy's most beautiful beaches, famous for its quartz-crystal sand—PuntAla Camp & Resort nestles within a protected natural area of hills, woods, and medieval villages. It serves as a sanctuary for those seeking to blend nature, adventure, and refined living, where outdoor experiences, environmental care, and sustainability form the core of every stay.

The campsite offers extensive activities for outdoor enthusiasts: hiking trails, MTB and gravel bike routes, sailing, SUP, and more.

## The owner is an ultra-distance racer

This vision springs from its owner, Thomas Daddi. **His passion for adventure has shaped a place that embodies immersive, regenerative vacation experiences.** Beyond being an entrepreneur, Thomas is an accomplished athlete who tackles demanding sports events. "In 2023, I participated in the Atlas Mountain Race, a 1,336 km course across the Atlas Mountains in Morocco," he recounts. His achievements include the Race Around Rwanda—1,000 km with 17,000 meters of elevation gain—completed alongside his sister, and The Mule Trail in Costa Rica in 2024, a 630 km challenge with 14,000 meters of climbing.

The value of his athletic feats lies not just in their impressive statistics, but in how they enhance the guest experience. His perspective as an entrepreneur-athlete shapes a vision beyond typical beach holidays. "While Tuscany may not offer such extreme adventures, and people here focus more on beauty, there are still plenty of opportunities to explore and challenge yourself."

## What riders find on site

His passion for active living enriches the experience for cycling enthusiasts. **Guests immerse themselves in nature without sacrificing comfort**, all while maintaining harmony with the environment. For those seeking responsible tourism that preserves the landscape, this is the answer. Their philosophy crystallizes in one simple truth: "Quality and environmental respect can only be guaranteed through temporariness." Even the accommodations—light, modern, and thoughtfully designed—follow strict sustainability principles. Bungalows and lodges, including the stylish Silverfield units (inspired by iconic 1960s American cylindrical trailers), use local materials and energy-efficient systems.

PuntAla Camp & Resort rejects fast, seasonal tourism with its typical caravans, verandas, and TVs. "Offering quality space," Thomas explains, "means providing proper equipment, a real workshop, a rental shop with high-end bikes, and satellite trackers for guest safety—essential since some valleys lack cell coverage."

## Self-sufficient by design

The property remains unfenced, allowing wildlife to roam freely. Human access to pristine areas is managed through elevated walkways and limited entry zones, as at Cala Violina. The Camp & Resort maintains independence from public utilities: "We're self-sufficient—treating seawater for all facility needs, including fire safety (a system the Region of Tuscany also uses in emergencies). We use low-pressure reverse osmosis, treat wastewater onsite, and reuse it for toilets and irrigation."

The Internet comes via satellite—"fiber is outdated"—while photovoltaic panels, thermal insulation, and passive housing solutions minimize energy consumption.

PuntAla Camp & Resort offers the perfect destination for nature and sport enthusiasts. Nestled between pine forest and sea, it features mobile homes, a pump track, sports courts, two restaurants, a supermarket, and a decade-long commitment to environmental practices, including plastic-free materials. Guests enjoy cashless payments, updated Komoot excursions, and a complete sailing school.

## From marshland to pine forest: 1927 to today

**PuntAla's story began in 1927** when Thomas's great-grandfather acquired the then-marshland. The 1950s saw land reclamation and maritime pine planting to absorb excess water—trees that still flourish today amid lush Mediterranean scrub. As vacations became accessible in the 1960s, demand grew through the 1970s, leading to facility developments in the 1980s and '90s. Thomas reflects, "PuntAla builds on that legacy. Today, as people seek authentic outdoor experiences, we offer excellent sports and nature activities."

## The events: Unpaved Roads, Grinduro and the rest

The Camp & Resort distinguishes itself by hosting high-level sporting events. Thomas has transformed his outdoor passion into a catalyst for local tourism, attracting international competitions. Cycling and sailing lead their offerings. "We aim to stay open year-round, hosting outdoor events during the off-season. Seeing these trails and landscapes—flowy yet challenging terrain—I recognized the potential. I knew a high-level race would put us on the map, so we developed accordingly."

The strategy succeeded. From the Sparviero Challenge (Italian SUP Championship) to the Finn World Master Championship and numerous cycling events, top athletes now compete here. Gravel biking thrives thanks to ideal terrain. The Camp & Resort hosts popular events like *Unpaved Roads*, *Fiorino Mud*—a 400 km Florence-to-Punta Ala round trip in one weekend—and *Grinduro*, an off-road enduro-style race.

Active regional participation means respecting and enhancing the territory while maintaining year-round operations. "These events don't just attract athletes—they create consistent visitor flow throughout the year. This keeps us integrated with the region, unlike seasonal operations." Beyond mere vacation, it's an immersive experience: internal trails, forested hills, charming villages, seaside cliffs, quality cuisine, and local hospitality. This Maremma region offers seasonal variations in colors, flavors, and scents. The mild climate supports year-round trekking, mountain biking, gravel riding, orienteering, and outdoor yoga.

## Resort or campsite?

Returning to our initial question—Resort or Camping? Both. "We provide diverse tourist accommodations—from lodging to experiences—prioritizing quality and local producer support. While accommodation drives our economy, the local impact multiplies. Trail maintenance benefits everyone: our guests, casual cyclists, mushroom foragers, hikers, and racers alike. We're developing a new bike path outside the campground and maintaining various tracks for biking, horseback riding, and hiking."

Thomas adds enthusiastically: "Unexpected partnerships emerge, even with groups focused on different aspects but sharing our commitment to preserving this beauty—like those maintaining the white roads."

Thomas's athletic achievements clearly influence his Camp & Resort vision. The facility promotes adventure and nature connection—ideals reflected in his competitive events. These experiences inform their approach to future challenges, particularly climate change resilience: rising seas, stronger winds, and storms. "As the environment evolves, architecture must adapt—protecting against fire, wind, and water. We're moving beyond vertical walls to geodesic structures that distribute forces evenly across all contact points."

These insights come from a CEO who recently completed *El Camino de Mulas*—conquering over 600 km and 10,000+ meters of elevation in 4 days, 9 hours, and 18 minutes, finishing at 4:18 p.m. on February 12, 2025.

So, would we buy a used car from him? Perhaps not. A bike? Likely. A vacation at his Camp & Resort? Without question.
